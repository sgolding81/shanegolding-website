Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 July 2026

This personal, static website exists to share projects and experiments. Nothing is sold here, and visitors cannot create accounts.

Information I collect

I do not run analytics, advertising trackers, profiling scripts, or application servers that collect visitor information.

If you email me, I receive the address and content you choose to send. I use that information to read and respond to your message and do not sell it.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not set tracking cookies. The Snake game stores one high-score value in your browser’s localStorage. It stays on your device, is never transmitted by this site, and can be removed through your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, for delivery, security, and abuse prevention under its own privacy terms.

Fonts and third-party requests

The site uses your device’s system fonts and does not request fonts from a third-party font service. Pages may contain links to other websites; their privacy practices apply once you follow those links.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to mail@shanegolding.net.
